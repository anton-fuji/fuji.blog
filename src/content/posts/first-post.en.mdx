## Starting this blog

Hi, I'm [**Fuji**](https://github.com/anton-fuji), a software engineer based in Osaka.

Recently, I have been keeping short daily notes in Notion. I write down what I thought about, what I tried, and what did not work.

At some point, I started wanting a place that felt more like my own platform.

Notion is convenient, but most of those notes stay private and rough. Writing in a blog format gives me a little more pressure to clarify what I understand, find the parts that are still vague, and leave a better trail for the future.

This blog will cover software, things I learn, and casual notes from everyday life.

Thanks for reading.
